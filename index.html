Frequently asked questions

Everything you should know.

What does ECHOBEN train students in?

ECHOBEN provides training in Cold Calling and Appointment Setting, Virtual Assistance, Customer Support and essential remote-work skills. The programme also covers professional communication, digital workplace tools, practical simulations, portfolio development, CV preparation, LinkedIn, job applications and interview preparation.

How many modules are included?

The programme contains 34 structured modules delivered through 68 guided teaching hours. The curriculum covers remote-work foundations, Cold Calling, Customer Support, Virtual Assistance, administrative skills and career readiness.

Do I need a laptop?

A laptop and reliable internet connection are strongly recommended for most remote-work activities.

Is the programme online?

Contact ECHOBEN to confirm the current Batch C delivery format, timetable and onboarding arrangements.

Will ECHOBEN guarantee me a job?

No. ECHOBEN provides training and career preparation, but employment, placement, client contracts and income are not guaranteed.

Does Option C require further payments?

Option C is a one-time ₦400,000 programme payment with no further earnings-sharing or programme remittances.

How do Options A and B work?

Option A involves ₦50,000 enrolment and 30% of monthly gross earnings for six consecutive months. Option B involves ₦100,000 enrolment and 10% of monthly gross earnings for six consecutive months. The written agreement must explain the calculation and conditions.

Will I receive a certificate?

Contact the academy to confirm its current certificate policy, eligibility requirements and issuing details before enrolment.

Can beginners apply?

Yes. The programme is designed for beginners as well as people who already have some experience.

Are simulated assignments the same as employment?

No. Simulations are training activities. Learners must not describe simulated work as paid employment or claim to have worked for a company without a genuine working relationship.

Policies and transparency

Know the terms before you commit.

These summaries explain important principles. Full written policies and agreements should be supplied before payment or onboarding.

Student Training Terms

Students should provide accurate information, participate respectfully, complete assigned activities and follow programme instructions. The full agreement should state programme obligations, duration, withdrawal and dispute procedures.

Payment Policy

All fees and earnings-sharing terms must be documented. Options A and B require a clear definition of gross earnings, reporting requirements, start and end dates, payment dates and treatment of periods without qualifying earnings. Option C is a one-time payment.

Privacy Policy

Application details should be used only for legitimate application and training administration purposes, protected against unauthorised access and retained only as necessary. A full privacy notice should explain the data controller, lawful basis, retention and contact process.

Refund Policy

Refund eligibility, deadlines, deductions and withdrawal procedures must be stated in a separate written policy before payment. Request the current policy from ECHOBEN.

Placement Disclaimer

Training and placement guidance do not guarantee employment, income, paid work or client contracts. Hiring decisions remain with employers and clients.

Professional Conduct

Learners must protect confidential information, respect privacy and avoid contacting real businesses or individuals for commercial activity without proper authorisation and supervision.

The final legal agreements and policies should be reviewed for compliance with applicable Nigerian law before accepting enrolment payments.
